---
title: Ask Vigie
description: Questions in plain words, answered from your telemetry with links to the evidence.
---

Set `VIGIE_AI_API_KEY` (`OPENAI_API_KEY` also works, and `VIGIE_AI_BASE_URL` points to any OpenAI-compatible provider) to turn on:

- **Ask Vigie.** Press **Ask** in the top bar and type "Why did checkout slow down this morning?" It answers from the read-only tools agents use over MCP, under your own role, cites trace ids, task ids and exact numbers, and links each one.
- **Explain.** On a log event or a trace, a short explanation of what happened, using your renderers and error renderers.
- **Search in words.** Type "failed emails to postwing yesterday" on **Logs**, and it becomes a filter you can see and edit.

The model is `gpt-4.1-mini` by default; set `VIGIE_AI_MODEL` to change it. What reaches the model is what the tools return, or the event you ask to explain, already masked at ingest. Without a key, the **Ask** button and the other two are hidden.
